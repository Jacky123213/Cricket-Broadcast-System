Information strip

Main is your free-text message. Add attendance, head-to-head or extra text below. Automatic rotation mixes these with the available match statistics.

Rotation includes last wicket, partnership runs (balls), occasional boundaries and one combined projection. In the final third of a chase, runs/balls needed and current/required run rates feature most often.

Graphics & match setup

Save setup changes before using the match controls. The first innings ends automatically at the over or wicket limit. The chase ends at its target or innings limit.

Take a graphic to air

Bowling card always follows the current bowling team, regardless of the innings selector.

Manual graphics remain on air until Reopen scoreboard, or until you select another graphic. Power Surge stays on independently in the current batting team's colour; press its button again to remove it. Automatic graphics do not replace a held manual graphic.

Starting the second innings swaps team colours and logos and clears the live score. The first innings stays in the match summary. A scorer reset plus team swap can also start it automatically.

Wicket details · optional correction

Bluetooth supplies the dismissed batter, runs/balls, wicket type, bowler and fielder automatically when those fields are sent. Caught, bowled, LBW, run-out and stumping have been verified. Stumping always says st wk, ignoring the fielder. An obstruction with six runs on that delivery says 6 and Out. Use this form only to correct a missing or incorrect detail; it does not add a wicket to the live score. Unknown dismissal codes display Out.
