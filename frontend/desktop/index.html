Camera does not appear
  1. Put the PC and device on the same normal Wi-Fi network.
  2. Install and trust the local certificate on Apple/Android.
  3. Open the camera page, allow camera permission, then choose the required lens.
  4. Avoid guest Wi-Fi and VPNs that block local devices.
Replay is buffering or has gaps

Start with 720p / 30 FPS, keep camera pages visible and test for at least 70 seconds. Reduce the number of cameras before increasing quality.

Scoreboard phone cannot find the PC

Use Generic (Enhanced) in Play-Cricket Scorer. Bluetooth must support peripheral mode. A growing packet count proves that data is arriving.

Overlay and DRS together

The same umpire camera can remain connected to DRS while its live view is used for production. Add the scoreboard overlay above that video source in OBS. DRS recording and overlay graphics share this server but remain separate layers.
