PC camera desk

Connect up to three cameras on this single page. Choose a different physical camera in each panel, test it, then connect. Close any older tabs using these same slots first: opening a slot twice replaces its connection.

PC slots use direct camera capture so background video does not depend on a zoom canvas being repainted. Built-in camera zoom remains available; digital zoom is off in these slots. Microphone access is optional. Keep this desk open, allow the site to stay active in your browser, and prevent Windows sleep. A fully suspended browser or stopped OBS Virtual Camera cannot supply new footage.
